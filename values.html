Our School Values

Heretaunga College is proudly embarking on an exciting new phase in our history, guided by a revitalized vision for our ākonga (learners). The School Board envisions a community centered around lifelong learning through powerful connections—connection to Our Place, Our People, Our Learning, and Our Future. Our historic school motto, "Kia Hiwa Rā," serves as our guiding call during this time of growth and improvement. It challenges our entire school community to remain watchful, alert, and entirely ready for the positive changes and opportunities that lie ahead.

This vision comes to life every day through our core HC values, which shape how we interact and grow together:

Kia Mataara (We Are Ready): We are present and prepared for learning.
Hiwa-i-te-rangi (We Aim High): We are motivated to succeed together.
Ratonga (We Lift Mana): We support and respect each other.
