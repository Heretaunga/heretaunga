What to Bring

Heading into high school, you’ll want to come prepared with the essentials that help you stay organized and succeed in your classes. Start with a sturdy backpack to carry your books, divide, lunch and water bottle. Make sure you’ve got writing tools like pens, pencils, highlighters, and erasers, plus a calculator for math and science. Having a laptop or tablet can make research and note-taking much easier, but the school offers chromebooks for hire if you do not have your own. For more information on stationary for each subject, refer to our official stationery lists.

Beyond academics, think about what you need to feel comfortable and confident throughout the day. A reusable water bottle keeps you hydrated, and having a few healthy snacks on hand can help you power through long afternoons. Pack personal hygiene items like hand sanitizer, tissues, and deodorant—you’ll thank yourself later. If you’re involved in sports or clubs, a change of clothes or gym gear might be necessary. And above all, bring a positive attitude, a curious mind, and a willingness to grow.
