Our School Library

The Heretaunga College School Library is a welcoming space for study, reading, and exploration. With a diverse collection of print and digital resources, students can access everything from novels to research tools. Quiet zones and collaborative areas cater to different learning styles, and helpful staff are always on hand for support


To use the Library's online software, and peruse our vast database of books, please visit Heretaunga College Online Library
